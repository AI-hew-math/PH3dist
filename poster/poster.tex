    \begin{block}{The big idea}
      \large
      Distance is a \textbf{tunable resolution knob} on musical structure.
      Sweeping \done${}\to{}$\dthree${}\to{}$\dtwo\ distills a piece down to its
      \textbf{core cyclic skeleton}.\par\vskip0.6ex
      \normalsize
      \begin{itemize}
        \item Three path distances on the music graph: \done\ (min edges),
              \dtwo\ (min weight, the only metric), \dthree\ (hybrid).
        \item Proven ordering \;$\textcolor{dcD2}{d_2}\!\le\!\textcolor{dcD3}{d_3}\!\le\!\textcolor{dcD1}{d_1}$\;
              and barcode injection $B_2\!\subseteq\!B_3\!\subseteq\!B_1$.
        \item Validated on real Korean court music (exact match to the paper's results).
      \end{itemize}
    \end{block}
  \end{column}
