# Geomungo (거문고) note samples

16 single-note geomungo samples (`geo_<MIDI>.wav`, MIDI 39–67 ≈ D#2–G4), segmented
from the **National Gugak Center "Digitaleum" (디지털음원)** 정악거문고 loud basic-scale
recordings (대현 / 유현 strings) by onset detection + pitch estimation.

- **Source:** National Gugak Center · Digitaleum — https://www.gugak.go.kr/digitaleum/
- **License:** KOGL Type-1 (Korea Open Government License) — free use including commercial,
  attribution required. Credit: 국립국악원 디지털음원 (National Gugak Center, Digitaleum).
- **Reproduce:** `python scripts/build_geomungo_samples.py`
- **Used by:** `ph_music/render_geomungo.py` to render the per-distance cycle-sonification clips.
